Call for Papers

We invite researchers from machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, human-computer interaction, cognitive science, recommender systems, AI safety, affective computing, education, healthcare, and related areas to submit work on Human Modeling and Cognitive Alignment in Interactive Agents.

Central Question

How should AI systems use uncertain models of humans and themselves to select interaction policies?

Topics of Interest

  • Human modeling, user modeling, theory of mind, personalization, preference learning, human simulation, and social reasoning

  • AI metacognition, uncertainty estimation, calibration, self-evaluation, capability assessment, selective prediction, and risk-aware reasoning

  • Cognitive alignment, interaction policies, clarification strategies, human-aware planning, human-AI collaboration, and shared mental models

  • Foundation models, multimodal systems, agentic AI, tool-using agents, memory systems, embodied agents, and robotics

  • AI companions, affective computing, trust, emotional modeling, long-term adaptation, and human-AI relationships

  • Evaluation methodologies, benchmarks, longitudinal studies, trust calibration, and human-centered metrics

  • Applications in education, healthcare, public health, scientific discovery, journalism, recommendation systems, accessibility, finance, and social robotics

Submission Types

  • Research papers

  • Position papers

  • Benchmark papers

  • Dataset papers

  • Systems papers

  • Interdisciplinary studies

Formatting

  • Main text up to 9 pages, excluding references and appendices.
  • References do not count toward the page limit.
  • Appendices are allowed, but reviewers are not required to read them.
  • Double-blind peer review.
  • Non-archival proceedings.
  • Authors retain the right to submit extended versions to archival venues.

Review Criteria

  • Relevance to the workshop theme

  • Scientific quality and technical soundness

  • Novelty and significance

  • Potential impact on human-AI interaction and agentic systems

  • Clarity of presentation

  • Reproducibility, benchmarking, or evaluation rigor where appropriate
